# Standard Deviation
> Standard deviation is essentially the same as variance, but with one important difference: it first calculates the variance of the data and then takes its square root.

**Formula:**

$$σ=\sqrt{Variance}$$

**Example:**

$$σ^2 = 66.66$$

$$σ = \sqrt{66.66}$$

$$σ = 8.164557550780079$$

**So:**

**Standard deviation:** $8.164557550780079$
