# Mean
> Well, the average is the same as the one from elementary school—it hasn't changed at all. By "average," we mean: if we add up all the values and divide them equally among the items, how much does each one get?

**Example:**

For example, the brightness intensity of each pixel is this:

`10 , 20 , 30 , 40 , 50`

Average brightness of each pixel:

$$Mean = \frac{10+20+30+40+50}{5}$$

Account:

$$Mean = \frac{150}{5} = 30$$

Thus, the average brightness of each pixel is $30$.
