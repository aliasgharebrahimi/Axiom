# Variance
> Variance tells us how far our data points are from their mean.

**Formula:**

$$s^2 = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}(x_i-\bar{x})^2}{N-1}$$

**That is:**

* **Find the mean $(μ)$**
* **Calculate the distance of each number from the mean**
* **Square the distances**
* **Find the mean of those squares**

**Example:**

**data** :

10 20 30

**Step1:** Average

$$μ = \frac{10 + 20 + 30}{3}$$

$$μ = 20$$

**Step2:** Distance from the mean


$10−20=−10$
$20−20=0$
$30−20=10$


**So:**

$−10,0,10$

**Step 3:** Square of the distances

$(−10)^2=100$

$0^2=0$

$10^2=100$

**So:**

$100,0,100$

**Step 4:** Calculating the average

$$Variance = \frac{100 + 0 + 100}{3}$$

$$Variance =  \frac{200}{3}$$

$$Variance = 66.66$$
