# Normal distribution
It states that in a dataset, numbers close to the mean are more frequent, and the frequency of numbers decreases as the distance from the mean increases.

**Formula:**

$$f(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}$$

Of course, for deep learning, there is no need to memorize formulas; instead, you need to have a solid understanding of these concepts:

* **$μ$ :** The data average, which is the center of the graph.
* **$σ$ :** It is the standard deviation of the data that determines the width of the curve.
* **$(x−μ)$ :** The distance of the data points from their mean.
