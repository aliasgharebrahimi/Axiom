# Probability
Probability is a numerical value that expresses the likelihood of an event occurring.

**Symbol:**

$$0≤P(A)≤1$$

**Formula:**

**Calculating the probability of an event occurring.**
$$P(A)=\frac{\text{Number of favorable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}}$$

The probability falls between these numbers: 0 means impossible, 1 means certain, and 0.5 means a fifty percent chance of occurrence.
